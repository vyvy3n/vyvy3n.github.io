Tianwei Yue

I am the CEO & Chief Scientist at Mathos AI, a Y Combinator (W24) backed startup building LLM agents for math education. I was named to Forbes 30 Under 30 (2025).

Previously, I was a researcher at AI Racing Tech (UC Berkeley), where our team won 3rd place at the Indy Autonomous Challenge 2023. My work on applying the Segment Anything Model to road perception received the Best Paper Award at CVPR 2024 Workshop.

I hold an M.S. in Computational Data Science from Carnegie Mellon University and a B.A. in Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong University, where I was admitted at age 14 through the Special Class for Gifted Young.
